How it works

This tool compares your Letterboxd ratings against each film's community average to get your hottest takes. It shows your top 10 — the 5 films you most underrated and the 5 you most overrated compared to everyone else. To run this comparison, your export is only used in your browser session and isn't stored anywhere afterward.

Step 1 — export your data

  1. Log into letterboxd.com
  2. Go to Settings → Data
  3. Click Export your data — this downloads a .zip file

Step 2 — upload it below

Drop the .zip exactly as downloaded from Letterboxd.
