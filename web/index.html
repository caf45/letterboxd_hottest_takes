How it works

This tool compares your Letterboxd ratings against each film's Letterboxd community average, then ranks your biggest disagreements — your hottest takes. Your export is only used in your browser session to run this comparison — it fetches each film's average rating from Letterboxd and isn't stored anywhere afterward.

Step 1 — export your data

  1. Log into letterboxd.com
  2. Go to Settings → Data
  3. Click Export your data — this downloads a .zip file

Step 2 — upload it below

You can drop the .zip exactly as downloaded, or just the ratings.csv file from inside it.
